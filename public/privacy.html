Draw Board: privacy

Last updated 13 October 2026

Draw Board is a whiteboard that runs in your browser at drawboardweb.netlify.app. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no server of its own that stores your drawings.

Your board

The board is saved in your browser's local storage, on your device. Files you save or open with Save / Open stay on your computer. Nothing is sent to us.

Google Drive (optional)

If you click the Google Drive button, Google asks you to let Draw Board use the drive.file permission. With it, Draw Board can see and change only the files it created itself: the folder draw_board_files in your Drive, the synced board board.svg in it, and the drawings you save there. It cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.

  • Your drawings travel directly between your browser and Google. They never pass through a server of ours.
  • The access Google grants is a short-lived token kept only in the open page's memory. It is discarded when you close the tab or choose Disconnect.
  • We do not collect, share or sell any data from your Google account, and do not use it for advertising or to train AI models.

Draw Board's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Hosting

The site's files are served by Netlify, which, like any web host, may log technical request data such as IP addresses.

Removing access and data

Choose Disconnect in the Drive dialog, and remove Draw Board under Google Account → Third-party connections. Delete the draw_board_files folder in Drive to remove the files. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes the local board.
